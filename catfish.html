CATFISH PHOTOMONTAGE

This project explores the absurdity of language by transforming familiar words into surreal, humorous visuals. Inspired by the way certain terms spark unexpected mental images, it brings these playful interpretations to life. By blurring the line between language and design, the project invites curiosity, amusement, and a fresh perspective on everyday words.

Utilizing various Photoshop tools such as clone stamp, healing brushes, dodge, and smudge, this project seamlessly merges cat heads with fish bodies. Efforts were focused on achieving a natural blend, refining details, and enhancing cohesion through background adjustments, photo filters, and noise for a polished final composition.
